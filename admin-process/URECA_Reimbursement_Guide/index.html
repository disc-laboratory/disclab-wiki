    FYP and URECA Reimbursement Guide¶

    A practical guide to purchase approvals, supporting documents and claim submission.

    Prepared: 18 September 2026. FYP instructions are based on the supplied 2025 procedure; URECA instructions are based on the public Project Consumable Fund section accessed on this date.

    1. Final Year Projects (FYP)¶

    Budget and forms¶

    The supplied procedure allocates $500 per FYP student, or $1,000 for a dual project. It refers to CEE laboratory processes; confirm their applicability with your laboratory administrator.

    Download the purchase requisition templates. Local copies are also available:

    • FYP Purchase Requisition form
    • Purchase Requisition Page 2 — Word
    • Purchase Requisition Page 1 — PDF
    • Purchase Requisition Page 2 — PDF

    Complete every highlighted field. Replace example names, dates, project numbers, academic years and amounts with your own details. The template includes the amount previously spent, the current purchase amount and the resulting total.

    Who receives the documents? The procedure says “me/myself” without naming the recipient. In this guide, laboratory administrator means that designated recipient; confirm the person with your laboratory before submitting.

    Route A — Equipment usage charges¶

    1. Complete the Purchase Request form, including all highlighted fields.
    2. Ask the laboratory technicians for the applicable equipment rates, then complete the equipment charging form.
    3. Obtain your Principal Investigator (PI) or FYP supervisor’s signature on the completed form.
    4. Submit the Purchase Request form and equipment charging form to the laboratory administrator for charging, filing and recording.

    The supplied materials do not identify a separate download link for the equipment charging form. Obtain it from the laboratory.

    Route B — Reimbursement for items you purchase¶

    1. Prepare the request. Complete the Purchase Request form and Purchase Requisition Page 2. Email Page 2 to your FYP supervisor for purchase approval.
    2. Compile purchase evidence. Attach receipts and bank statements. Ensure the items and amounts match Purchase Requisition Page 2.
    3. Obtain the supervisor’s signature. After purchasing, have your FYP supervisor or PI sign the forms.
    4. Obtain laboratory endorsement before claiming. Send all Purchase Request forms, receipts and bank statements to the laboratory administrator. The administrator will arrange the Laboratory Faculty-in-Charge’s signature and email the endorsed forms back to you.
    5. Submit the claim in ICS. Once the endorsed forms have been returned, access the Integrated Claims System (ICS) through INTU and enter the details below.
    6. Send documents for filing. After submitting the ICS claim, email the Purchase Request form and equipment charging form, where relevant, to the laboratory administrator for filing and recording.

    Do not submit the ICS claim before the Laboratory Faculty-in-Charge has signed the forms.

    Route C — Purchase through NTU Ariba¶

    1. Complete the Purchase Request form and all highlighted fields.
    2. Complete Purchase Requisition Page 2 and email it to your FYP supervisor for approval.
    3. Provide the laboratory administrator with a supplier quotation for non-catalogue purchases, or a screenshot of the items in the NTU catalogue for catalogue purchases.
    4. For chemicals, email the Material Safety Data Sheets (MSDS) and other documents to the CEE Safety Committee before sending the Purchase Request forms to the administrator. Tell the administrator about hazards, such as corrosiveness, flammability or toxicity, so suitable storage can be prepared.
    5. Obtain your PI’s or FYP supervisor’s signature on the completed form.
    6. Submit the Purchase Request form and equipment charging form, where relevant, to the laboratory administrator for processing and records.

    Additional approval: The procedure says purchases exceeding $500 require the Associate Chair (Academic)’s signature. The requisition template instead refers to total spending exceeding $500 for a single project or $1,000 for a dual project. Confirm the applicable threshold with the laboratory administrator before proceeding, especially for dual projects.

    Source: FYP Procedure 2025. Missing steps may cause rejection or delay.

    2. URECA Projects¶

    Before purchasing¶

    • Budget: $500 excluding GST per academic year for URECA, IDR-URECA and FYP-URECA. Purchases: August–May.
    • Obtain URECA Office pre-approval; unapproved purchases are unfunded.
    • Prefer purchase orders (PO). Reimbursement covers survey/fieldwork or purchases unavailable through PO. CLRI-URECA purchases go through schools/labs/centres.
    • Use project-related consumables; check existing university resources first. Purchased items remain NTU property.

    Application and submission¶

    1. Discuss purchases with your supervisor.
    2. Read the detailed submission procedures. Routes include PO, corporate-card PO, corporate PayNow participant payments, receipt reimbursement and participant-fee reimbursement.
    3. Apply through the URECA system; submit the appropriate claim/pre-approval form for verification.
    4. Follow up with your supervisor; send the verified form and supporting documents to your project school’s admin/general office.

    Limits and deadlines¶

    • Participant payments: $45 per participant overall; photocopying: $100 per project.
    • Check eligible expenses, exclusions and documentation conditions before buying.
    • The published deadline is 31 May 2026, 23:59, already past. Confirm the applicable deadline with URECA Office.
    • Outstanding poster/abstract submissions block claim access.
    • Return purchased items through your supervisor when the project ends.
